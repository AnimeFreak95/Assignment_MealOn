# 🚀 MealsOn Phase 1 Pilot Launch Plan

> **Strategic Mission:** Validate product-market fit, habit loop adherence, and batch unit economics in a dense, high-affinity corporate corridor before capital-intensive metro expansion.

---

## 📍 1. Beachhead Cluster Selection & Justification

### Selected Beachhead: **DLF Cyber Park & Cyber City, Sector 24/25, Gurgaon**
- **Geographic Radius:** 3.5 km core cluster (well within the 7 km hybrid geofence).
- **Target Demographic:** High-density IT/consulting corporate professionals (Google, American Express, EY, Cognizant, Zomato tech offices).
- **Estimated Cluster Workforce:** ~45,000 corporate desk employees with hybrid work patterns (3 days office, 2 days WFH).

### Why this Beachhead?
1. **Hyper-Concentrated Corporate Towers:** 3 anchor corporate towers alone house over 18,000 professionals, maximizing lobby drop batch density.
2. **High Existing Food Delivery Friction:** Average on-demand delivery time is 42–55 minutes during 1:00 PM lunch rush with ₹48–₹65 packaging and delivery surcharges.
3. **Proximity to Cloud Kitchen Hubs:** Udyog Vihar Phase 4 & Sector 22 kitchen clusters are located within 3–4 km, ensuring fresh transit in under 12 minutes.

## 🍳 3. Kitchen Partner Onboarding & SLAs

To guarantee taste consistency, nutritional transparency, and 100% on-time fulfillment, kitchen partners operate under strict contractual SLAs:

| Partner Dimension | Requirement & SLA Standard | Enforcement Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kitchen Footprint** | FSSAI certified, dedicated packing bay for MealsOn | Site audit before contract sign-off |
| **Morning Manifest Lock** | Final meal production counts dispatched by **10:05 PM** night before; kitchen cooking starts from locked sheet at 6:00 AM | Zero same-day cancellations tolerated |
| **Dispatch Handover SLA** | Food packed in tamper-proof thermal boxes and handed to MealsOn fleet by **11:45 AM** | Penalty of ₹50 per 10-minute dispatch delay |
| **Packaging Quality** | 100% biodegradable, spill-proof, microwave-safe containers with macro/allergen labels | Weekly mystery audits & CSAT tracking |
| **Wholesale Margin** | 25% wholesale discount off standard on-demand menu prices | Guaranteed high-volume daily batch orders |

## 🛵 4. Fleet Logistics & Delivery Execution

### The "Single-Spoke" Batch Delivery Model
Unlike on-demand delivery where 1 rider delivers 1 food parcel (₹55–₹65 cost), MealsOn deploys dedicated **Cluster Fleet Runners**:
1. **11:45 AM – 12:15 PM:** Runner sweeps the 5 kitchen hubs in a 2 km loop with thermal cargo panniers (capacity: 25 meal boxes).
2. **12:20 PM:** Runner arrives at the designated corporate tower lobby desk.
3. **12:30 PM – 2:00 PM:** Tower Lobby Desk Steward stages boxes into Slot A, Slot B, and Slot C holding racks.
4. **Push Notification:** Automated WhatsApp/App notification triggers: *"Your lunch has arrived at DLF Cyber Park Lobby Desk A (Bin #14). Enjoy your warm meal!"*

### Delivery Cost Waterfall (Per Meal Drop)
- **Rider Transit Cost (Batched):** ₹14.00
- **Lobby Staging / Dispatch Cost:** ₹6.00
- **Thermal Bag & Equipment Amortization:** ₹4.00
- **Total MealsOn Drop Cost:** **₹24.00** *(vs. ₹65.00 on-demand = 63% savings)*

At the end of Week 6, the product leadership team will evaluate the pilot against four strict criteria before greenlighting Phase 2 expansion:

| Success Metric | Pilot Target (Week 6) | Failure / Pivot Threshold | Strategic Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Fleet Drop Cost** | **&le; ₹26.00** per meal | &gt; ₹35.00 | Validates the unit economic thesis of 63% logistics savings. |
| **Month-1 Pass Renewal Rate** | **&ge; 70%** (organic or prompted) | &lt; 50% | Validates that zero-breakage rollover and Habit Tree unlock true LTV. |

## 🎯 6. Pilot Risk Assessment & Mitigation Strategies

1. **Risk: Building Security Restrictions on Lobby Delivery**
   - *Mitigation:* Secure pre-authorized MoUs with DLF Commercial Property Management to install designated MealsOn smart pickup stations in the reception atrium.
2. **Risk: Sudden Rainy Season Transit Delays**
   - *Mitigation:* Dispatch buffer window increased by 20 minutes on rainy days; kitchen handoff shifted from 11:45 AM to 11:25 AM.
3. **Risk: User Confusion Between Home and Office Routing**
   - *Mitigation:* 8:00 PM Smart Cut-Off Drawer explicitly highlights tomorrow's destination badge (e.g. `Delivering to: DLF Cyber Park 🏢`) with a 1-tap "Flip to Home 🏠" toggle before the 10:00 PM lock.
