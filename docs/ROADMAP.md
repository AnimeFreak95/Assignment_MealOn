## 🛡️ 5. Strategic Trade-Offs & Roadmap Guardrails

To protect unit economics and product reliability, three inviolable guardrails govern the roadmap:
1. **Predictability Over Spontaneity (3 Fixed 30-Min Windows):** Rejected open 15-minute on-demand slots. Batching 10–14 lunches into `12:30–1:00`, `1:00–1:30`, and `1:30–2:00` collapses drop costs by 63% (from ₹65 to ₹24).
2. **Night-Before Discipline (10 PM Kitchen Lock):** Rejected morning cancellations. Gives partner cloud kitchens exact prep manifests by 10:05 PM, eliminating 28% food waste and enabling wholesale pricing.
3. **Cluster Integrity (7 km Geofence & 10 PM Cutoff):** Hybrid office-to-home address flips must remain within a 7 km radius of the anchor hub and be locked by 10 PM the night before, completely stopping split-fleet deadhead transit.
