## 🚀 2. The 3 Phased Rollouts

### 🟢 Phase 1: Pilot MVP (Weeks 1–6) — *“The Core Habit & Trust Engine”*
**Goal:** Deliver a flawless lunch experience for 500 users across 3 corporate towers in DLF Cyber Park.
- **Pass & Payments:** Upfront (+5% bonus), UPI AutoPay at 10 PM, and corporate meal cards.
- **Kitchen Lock & 30-Min Drop:** 10 PM cutoff enables 3 batch cooking waves; ₹24 batch delivery.
- **Zero-Breakage Wallet:** 100% rollover protection on skipped meals so money is never lost.
- **Food Vault & Hybrid Routing:** 12–20 trusted meals on rotation with 1-tap Office vs. Home toggle.
- **Habit Tree:** 5-stage living bonsai with 4/5 flexible rule (1 protected skip) for weekly retention.

---

### 🟡 Phase 2: Cluster Expansion (Weeks 7–14) — *“Density & Social Co-Op”*
**Goal:** Expand to 12 tech towers + 8 residential pods (2,500 subscribers) to maximize route density.
- **Tower Tribe Pod Co-Op:** Co-workers pool orders to unlock collective Friday Fuel credits (₹25/₹50/₹75).
- **AI Playlist Engine:** Dynamic dietary prompt search for subscribers wanting exploratory menus.
- **Corporate Direct Billing:** B2B integration with corporate HR wellness programs.

---

### ⚪ Phase 3: Metro Scale (Months 4+) — *“Intelligence & Full Automation”*
**Goal:** Expand across Bengaluru (ORR) and Mumbai (BKC) to reach 10,000+ daily orders.
- **Calendar Auto-Sync:** Automated WFH detection via Google / Microsoft OAuth.
- **Lobby Warming Pods:** Temperature-controlled smart pickup locker units in major office atriums.
- **Dedicated 100% EV Delivery Fleet:** Net-zero carbon batch logistics.

---

## 💡 3. Guiding Prioritisation Principles

1. **Predictability Over Spontaneity:** We prioritize 10 PM batch order locking over instant on-demand changes because it cuts food waste to under 3% and drops delivery cost from ₹65 to ₹24.
2. **Control Over Algorithms:** We ship the **Food Vault** first because users want their 10–15 trusted comfort meals. AI discovery is an optional layer, not the default.
3. **Zero Guilt, 100% Retention:** We prioritize **Pause Shield & Rollover** over aggressive lock-ins. Treating unconsumed meals fairly is our #1 weapon against subscription churn.
