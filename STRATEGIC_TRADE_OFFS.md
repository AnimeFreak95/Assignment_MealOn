## 🧭 1. Executive Trade-Off Philosophy

Traditional on-demand food delivery platforms maximize **tactical flexibility** (order anything, anytime, to any doorstep in 20 minutes) at the expense of **crushing unit economics** (₹55–₹70 last-mile courier cost), **high kitchen waste** (20–28%), and **daily cognitive decision fatigue** (18–22 minutes wasted scrolling menus).

**MealsOn takes the opposite stance:** We deliberately constrain micro-flexibility to unlock **macro-predictability**. This predictability slashes delivery costs by **63%**, reduces kitchen food waste to **<3%**, and automates weekday lunch—allowing us to fund **100% free delivery** and **complimentary Friday rewards** sustainably.

## 🎨 3. Product & UX Decision Trade-Offs

| # | Product/UX Decision | What We Sacrificed (The Cost) | What We Gained (Psychological / User Rationale) | Mitigation Strategy |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P1** | **AI Auto-Pilot Default (Passive) vs. Daily Active Ordering** | **Granular daily control:** User doesn't hand-pick every individual dish each morning. | **Zero Daily Decision Fatigue:** Corporate employees waste 18–22 mins daily debating what to eat. Auto-pilot converts weekday lunch into background infrastructure. | **Curated Food Vault** gives users full veto power over allowed meals, with instant 1-tap swaps at the 8:00 PM cut-off. |
| **P2** | **5-Day Mon–Fri Bounded Loop vs. Infinite Streaks** | **The vanity flex:** No 60-day or 100-day continuous badge numbers (like Snapchat or Duolingo). | **Prevents "streak-death" churn:** When users break a 45-day streak due to a sick day, they feel defeated and abandon the app. A 5-day cycle resets every Monday, offering a fresh, achievable slate. | Cumulative milestone badges track macro consistency (e.g., *"4-Week Clean Streak Club"*). |
| **P3** | **Office Pod Smart Locker Drop vs. Doorstep / Desk Delivery** | **White-glove desk handover:** The rider does not bring the meal to your specific chair on Floor 4. | **Zero security friction & guaranteed delivery:** Tech parks ban riders from elevators/floors. Lobby/Smart Locker drops eliminate 15-minute entrance delays and cold food. | Automated SMS/WhatsApp alert with direct locker OTP sent right when the batch lands. |
| **P4** | **Duality of Choice (2 Treats) vs. Open Catalog Credit** | **Infinite reward choice:** Users cannot pick literally anything from a 1,000-item marketplace catalog. | **Decisive gratification:** Offering 2 polarized choices (*Clean Fuel: Protein Bar* vs *Cheat Treat: Cold Brew*) gives autonomy without re-introducing decision fatigue. | Monthly rotation of treat pairs (e.g., Donut vs. Kombucha, Cheesecake vs. Detox Cold-Pressed Juice). |
| **P5** | **Pre-Set Hybrid Location Matrix vs. Daily Address Prompts** | **Dynamic daily location queries:** The app doesn't ask *"Where are you eating today?"* each morning. | **Frictionless hybrid routine:** Mon/Tue/Thu automatically map to Office Pod; Wed/Fri map to Home, reflecting 3-2 corporate hybrid reality. | **Two Economics Guardrails:** (1) **7 km Cluster Geofence:** Home must be ≤ 7 km from the office kitchen cluster for ₹0 delivery; (2) **10 PM Routing Lock:** Office/Home switch locks at 10:00 PM night-before; zero same-day morning flips. |
